Our Team

The advocates behind SSR & Associates — a trusted law firm in Srinagar serving clients across Kashmir with dedication and integrity.

Syed Sibtain Razvi
Founder & Managing Partner
Syed Sibtain Razvi is the Founder & Managing Partner of the firm, enrolled with the Bar Council of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. He leads the firm's civil, criminal, and constitutional practice, appearing regularly before the Hon'ble High Court of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, the Subordinate Courts, and various Tribunals.
Mohammad Rizwan
Co-Founder & Partner
Mohammad Rizwan is the Co-Founder & Partner of the firm, bringing considerable experience in criminal, property, corporate, and arbitration matters. He advises clients on criminal defence, land and revenue disputes, commercial contracts, and dispute resolution, working closely with clients to safeguard their interests across Kashmir.
Hakeem Tehjud
Associate
Hakeem Tehjud is an Associate Advocate with the firm, supporting litigation, legal drafting, and client consultations. His drafting work spans writ petitions, CAT petitions, suits, agreements, affidavits, and case briefs, while he represents clients before the High Court, Subordinate Courts, and Tribunals.
