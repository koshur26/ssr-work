Syed Sibtain Razvi
Founder & Managing Partner

Syed Sibtain Razvi is the Founder and Managing Partner of the firm, with a strong litigation practice built on extensive courtroom experience and strategic legal advocacy. While his practice spans civil, constitutional, and commercial litigation, he is particularly known for his distinguished criminal law practice. He regularly appears in complex criminal trials, bail matters, writ proceedings, and other complex litigations before the High Court of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, the Subordinate Courts, and various Tribunals. He advises and represents individuals, businesses, and institutions, delivering practical legal solutions and effective representation across Jammu & Kashmir.
